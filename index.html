Cultural Lore

Welcome to the heritage of Shahdol, Madhya Pradesh.

Bamboo is known as "Green Gold." For indigenous tribes like the Baiga and Gond, it is central to life, used from cradle to grave.

Before weaving, a true artisan knows that the soul of the Tokri lies in the preparation of the Kamchis (splits). The moisture of the bamboo dictates its strength and flexibility.
